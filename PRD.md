# Premium Elevators — Website Product Requirements

## Purpose
Present Premium Elevators' lifts and services to homeowners, builders, hospitals, commercial properties, and industrial facilities in Ahmedabad and Baroda (Vadodara). Help visitors find an appropriate category and contact the company. Search visibility is a priority; no #1 ranking can be guaranteed by site code alone.

## Brand and visual rules
- Company name: Premium Elevators. The supplied logo is the source brand mark; a monochrome adaptation is used in the interface to honour the strict palette.
- Allowed interface colours only: background `#ececec`, heading/primary foreground `#274A66`, regular text Tailwind gray-700 `#374151`. No additional interface colours, including accent colours. Photographs are desaturated to support this direction.
- Geist Regular for typography. Headings weight 400 and full opacity. Body copy 12px, weight 400 and full opacity. No eyebrow labels, explanatory subheadings, or subtext directly below section headings. Use Hugeicons only for illustrative UI icons.
- Minimal, spacious, approachable layouts. Avoid excessive shadows. Pages and sections target a viewport-height composition when content permits, while remaining scrollable without clipping on smaller screens. Responsive on phone, tablet, and desktop.

## Pages and navigation
- Home: branded image-first introduction, featured lift types, service list, and contact action.
- About us: grounded company introduction; do not invent history, certifications, counts, or guarantees.
- Lifts: overview of home, passenger, hospital, goods, capsule, and hydraulic lifts. Each has a detail page with a localized `-in-ahmedabad-or-baroda` slug, relevant H1, body copy, internal links and contact action.
- Services: overview and detail pages for after-sales service, design & installation support, maintenance & repair, and modernization. Services hover menu shows All services plus every option; mobile menu exposes every option.
- Gallery: clearly mark generated illustrative images as illustrative, not company projects.
- Contact: direct email details, provided addresses, and a form that composes an email in the visitor's email application; it does not claim to send automatically.
- Privacy policy and terms & conditions: concise website notices, subject to legal review before production.

## Search and content requirements
- Canonical domain: `https://premiumelevators.in`. Unique route-level titles, descriptions, canonical and Open Graph tags. One H1 per page. Descriptive internal links and image alt text.
- Target human search intent such as home lifts in Ahmedabad, home lifts in Baroda, passenger lifts, hospital lifts, goods lifts, and local lift maintenance. Avoid keyword stuffing and copied competitor claims.
- Authority references can link outward to relevant primary sources such as BIS and the Gujarat government. These outbound links are **not** backlinks; actual inbound links require independent publisher relationships and cannot be generated by the website.
- A public sitemap should be considered at launch when the final domain is serving the site. Keep the domain and page URLs consistent.
- Search ranking, AI citations and organic traffic require ongoing measurement and publishing; the website alone cannot promise #1 rankings.
